# Career_OS Functional Route Taxonomy v1.0

Functional route classification is separate from base-resume selection. Three functional routes exist, but exactly two maintained production bases exist.

## Commercial / Research

Primary direction. Aligned families include Strategy Analyst, Business Analyst, Growth or Commercial Strategy Analyst, Market or Competitive Intelligence Analyst, Business Planning Analyst, Management Associate, Business/Data/Insights/Operations/Reporting Analyst, Customer or Marketing Analytics Analyst, and business-facing transformation roles.

Default base: `COMMERCIAL_PRIMARY`.

## Corporate Finance / Transaction / Advisory

Secondary direction. Aligned families include Financial or FP&A Analyst, Commercial/Corporate Finance Analyst, Valuation Analyst, Treasury Analyst, Management Reporting Analyst, Corporate/Commercial/Transaction Banking Analyst, junior Credit/Risk roles, and support-level transaction or advisory roles.

Default base: `CF_TRANSACTION_SECONDARY`.

KYC, AML, financial-crime, regulatory, compliance, risk-ownership, and independent advisory claims remain evidence gaps unless explicitly verified.

## Markets / FMI / FinTech

Selective/stretch direction. Aligned families may include junior markets research, market infrastructure, exchange/clearing/settlement, payments, middle-office, asset-management research, and business-facing FinTech roles.

This route never creates a third maintained base. It selects the nearest evidence base using responsibilities and skills:

- Research, commercial analysis, strategy, customer/market insight → `COMMERCIAL_PRIMARY`
- Finance, banking, transaction, valuation, markets, middle-office, or investment analysis → `CF_TRANSACTION_SECONDARY`

Pure software engineering and machine-learning engineering remain outside scope.

## Title normalization and seniority

Original `Role_Title` is immutable. `Normalized_Role_Title` is a separate routing field, and title is never the sole routing signal.

Controlled seniority categories remain Intern, Graduate, Entry Level, Analyst, Junior Associate, Associate, Senior Associate, Manager, Senior Manager, Director, and Executive. Primary targets are Graduate through Junior Associate, with selective Associate review.
