# Career_OS Factual Integrity Rules v0.1

## Permanent rule

Resume tailoring and application support may reframe verified facts, but must never invent or promote unsupported information.

Never invent:

- skills or software proficiency
- certifications or professional licenses
- metrics or outcomes
- ownership or responsibilities
- leadership or people management
- languages or proficiency levels
- industry experience
- regulatory or compliance responsibility
- investment recommendations or project outcomes
- technical methods

Unknown values stay blank/null or are classified `UNKNOWN`. A missing job requirement stays a gap; it must not be inserted into the Candidate Profile or resume.

## Ownership controls

Support-level evidence remains support-level. Projects with financing scale describe projects supported, not money personally raised, secured, arranged, or led.

## Source hierarchy

Tier 1 verified records control Tier 2 Candidate Profile; Tier 2 controls Tier 3 frozen Base Resume interpretation; lower application and outcome records cannot overwrite higher-tier facts automatically.

Synthetic test facts may exist only in `test_jobs/` and `test_results/` and must never enter the Candidate Profile.
